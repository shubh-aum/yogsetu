How it works for teachers

1. Create your profile. Add your bio, photos, and upload your certification documents for verification.

2. Get verified. Our team checks your credentials with the issuing body. Most reviews complete within a few days.

3. Set your availability and pricing. Per-session, package pricing, or both — you decide.

4. Receive requests, browse requirements. Approve the connections that fit; apply to open requirements that match what you teach.

5. Move to email, then WhatsApp. Once approved, you talk directly. Payment for sessions happens off-platform.
