About Teacher

About Priya Sharma

Priya Sharma is a passionate and dedicated RYT 500 certified yoga teacher based in New Delhi, with over 8 years of experience guiding students of all ages and abilities. Her journey with yoga began during a period of personal healing from a sports injury, which led her to discover the profound therapeutic potential of mindful practice.

Priya specialises in Hatha and Vinyasa yoga with a deep emphasis on therapeutic applications for injury recovery, chronic pain management, and stress relief. She has worked with over 1,200 students ranging from complete beginners to advanced practitioners, corporate professionals, pregnant women, and senior citizens. Her classes are known for their warmth, precision in alignment cues, and the balance they strike between physical challenge and deep relaxation. Priya completed her advanced training in Rishikesh under Yoga Alliance International and holds additional certifications in Prenatal Yoga and Yoga Therapy.

Philosophy

Teaching Philosophy

"Yoga is not about touching your toes. It is about what you learn on the way down. Every body is different, every journey is unique, and my role is to hold the space for yours."

I believe yoga is a lifelong conversation between the mind, body, and breath. My sessions focus on understanding each student's unique constitution, limitations, and aspirations. I teach yoga as a complete system — not just physical postures, but breath awareness, mental clarity, and the subtle joy of presence.
