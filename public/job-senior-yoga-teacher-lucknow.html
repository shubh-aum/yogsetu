About This Role

Job Description

Ananda Wellness Studio is looking for a passionate, experienced Senior Yoga Teacher to join our growing studio in Gomti Nagar, Lucknow. This is a full-time in-studio role with excellent growth potential and benefits.

As a Senior Yoga Teacher, you will lead a variety of group and private classes, mentor junior instructors, contribute to curriculum design, and play an active role in building a thriving wellness community. You'll work closely with our Studio Head to develop premium class formats that align with our holistic philosophy.

We serve a diverse student base — from beginner adults and working professionals to advanced practitioners and seniors. Your ability to adapt and personalise instruction will be central to this role.

Your Duties

Responsibilities

  • Lead 3–5 group yoga sessions per day (Hatha, Vinyasa, Pranayama, Yoga Nidra)
  • Conduct private and semi-private classes for individual clients
  • Develop and update monthly class schedules in collaboration with the Studio Head
  • Design and deliver 4-week and 8-week progressive yoga programs
  • Mentor and support 2–3 junior instructors at the studio
  • Maintain student records, progress notes, and health screening forms
  • Engage with corporate wellness clients for occasional outreach sessions
  • Participate in YogKulam's digital content creation (short videos, live sessions)
  • Handle student inquiries and on-board new members with trial sessions
  • Uphold studio standards, safety protocols, and the holistic wellness philosophy

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to apply?

+

A valid yoga certification (minimum RYT 200), your resume/CV, and a brief cover letter describing your teaching philosophy. Documents can be uploaded in PDF or DOC format.

Is there a trial class before final selection?

+

Yes. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a 45-minute demo class at the studio before the final interview. This is a normal part of the yoga hiring process — no need to be nervous!

Do I need corporate yoga experience?

+

Corporate yoga experience is a plus but not mandatory. Studio teaching experience of 3+ years is the primary requirement. You'll be trained in the corporate module once onboarded.

Is this role open to teachers outside Lucknow?

+

Yes, candidates willing to relocate to Lucknow are welcome. A relocation allowance of up to ₹5,000 is provided for candidates moving from other cities.

What is the interview process?

+

1) Application review (1–2 days) → 2) Phone/video screening (15 min) → 3) Demo class at studio (45 min) → 4) Final interview with Studio Head → 5) Offer letter. The whole process typically takes 5–7 days.
