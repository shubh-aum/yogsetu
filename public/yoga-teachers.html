FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the right yoga teacher for me?▾

Use the search bar at the top of this page. Filter by your city, preferred yoga style, class format (online or home visit), experience level, and language. Each teacher profile has a bio, certifications, reviews, and pricing. You can also book a free 30-minute demo before committing to any package.

Are all teachers on YogKulam certified?▾

Yes. Every teacher holds a recognised certification from Yoga Alliance (RYT 200 or RYT 500), IAYT, KRI, Birthlight, or an equivalent body. Our team manually verifies each document before the profile is published. The certification type is shown on every teacher card with a verified badge.

How do online yoga classes work?▾

After booking, the teacher confirms the time and sends a Zoom link via WhatsApp or email. You need a stable internet connection and a yoga mat. Sessions are live and interactive — the teacher can see and correct your form in real time. Recordings are available on request.

What if I want home visit yoga sessions?▾

Filter by "Offline" or "Both" in Class Type and search by your city. Each teacher lists their coverage area on their profile. Travel charges may apply for longer distances — agreed directly before the first booking.

How much do yoga sessions cost?▾

Session rates are set by each teacher and displayed on every card (typically ₹300–₹800/session). Most teachers also offer monthly packages (8–12 sessions) at a reduced rate with progress tracking and WhatsApp support. The first demo session is always free — no payment required.

Can I try a free class before paying?▾

Absolutely. Every teacher offers a free 30-minute demo for new students. Click "Book Demo" on any card, fill in your details, and the teacher will reach out to schedule. No payment card is required.

Can I book a teacher for corporate wellness sessions?▾

Yes. Filter by "Corporate Yoga" to see teachers experienced in workplace wellness. Sessions can be regular group classes (online or on-site), weekly programmes, or special events. Teachers provide customised proposals for your team size and goals. Contact us on WhatsApp for group pricing.
