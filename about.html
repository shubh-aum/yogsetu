About YogSetu

A directory built on proof, not profiles

YogSetu is a YogKulam initiative — connecting students with yoga teachers whose credentials have actually been checked.

Why we built this

Most people find a yoga teacher through a random agency, a contact's recommendation, or an Instagram reel — with no way to check whether the training claimed is real. YogSetu exists to fix that one problem: proof, before you commit to a teacher.

Every teacher on YogSetu is verified directly with the body that issued their certification — not just asked to upload a photo of a certificate. That badge expires every year and has to be re-earned, so a listing always reflects a currently-valid credential.

Backed by YogKulam

YogSetu is built and credentialed by YogKulam, a yoga teacher training institute running for over a decade, with 40,000+ certified teachers and 60,000+ students trained. The verification infrastructure behind YogSetu is the same one YogKulam uses to certify its own teachers.

How we think about trust

No in-platform payment collection, no lock-in, no algorithmic ranking based on who pays the most. Teachers keep their pricing, students keep the direct relationship, and YogSetu's only job is to make sure the credential in front of you is real.

Verify, don't trust
